And Interop: the Mullet Under the Mullet Every few years crypto gets its moment in the spotlight. For most of its life, those moments followed a predictable four year cycle: prices shot up, usually too far, people piled in, most got burned, and a few stayed. That’s how crypto grew, by booms and busts that left behind true believers.
