A Primer One of the interesting ideas in crypto right now is something called resource locks, which flips the mental model of how we as users see the crypto space from a chain first model to an asset first model. This is the mental model we’ve all internalized: Today’s UX: chain first This may have worked well in crypto’s earlier days, when there were only a handful of chains and wallets to worry about. But today, steps (2) and (3) in the process above have become far more confusing.
